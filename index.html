GroupWhy
The same digits mean different numbers in different countries.
Open the app
1,234 is 1234 in the US and 1.234 in Germany. 1.234 is the other way round. 1 234,5 is a valid French number and an invalid English one.

What it does

  • Takes a number as written and shows how 33 locales would read it, grouped by outcome.
  • Shows each locale's group and decimal characters with their code points (space, no-break space, narrow no-break space and apostrophe all look alike).
  • Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How far to trust it

  • Symbols come from the browser's own Intl data. Compared with Python Babel 2.18 on 4342 strings in 29 locales with no mismatch. In de-AT, de-CH, fr-CH and ar-EG the two data sets use different separators, so those four disagree. The reader is strict and does not guess like some apps do.
